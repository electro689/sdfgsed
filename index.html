Our Story

A Space Built for Families, Flavours & Memories

Nestled in the heart of Undri, Pune — Krushna Pure Veg Family Restaurant is a place where good food meets good company. We serve a diverse multi-cuisine menu under one roof, all cooked in a strictly vegetarian kitchen.

Whether you're here for a casual family lunch, a birthday dinner, or a large gathering in our banquet hall — we aim to make every visit comfortable, nourishing, and memorable. Our space includes AC family dining, poolside seating, and ample parking — making us one of the more complete dining destinations in the Undri area.
